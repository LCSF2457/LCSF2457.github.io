Our Story

Antonio's Fire Pits was founded in 2010 with a simple yet passionate vision: to bring people together around a beautiful, functional fire. What started as a small, family-run operation quickly gained popularity due to its dedication to high-quality craftsmanship and unique designs. Our founder, Antonio Stefan, initially handcrafted each fire pit in his garage, carefully designing them to provide warmth, style, and durability. The focus on creating products that seamlessly blend aesthetics with function soon earned the company a loyal following.


As demand grew, so did the company, transitioning from a small local business to a nationally recognized name in the outdoor furniture industry. Today, Antonio's Fire Pits offers a diverse range of fire pits, each crafted with precision and attention to detail. Our commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction has allowed us to remain at the forefront of the industry, continuously evolving to meet the changing needs of our customers. From cozy backyards to large outdoor gatherings, Antonio's Fire Pits has become a symbol of warmth and connection, making memories around the fire a reality for families and friends everywhere.

Did you know?

Antonio's Fire Pits began as a passion project in Antonio Stefan's garage, where he crafted the first fire pit by hand. What started with just one design has since evolved into a collection of over 30 unique fire pit styles, all crafted to perfection!
